samsync

Privacy Policy · Last updated November 18, 2026

samsync is a small personal tool run by Sam Raphael for himself and his family. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public.

What it does

When a person likes a video on YouTube, samsync saves that video as a bookmark in the same person's own Raindrop.io account.

What data it accesses

  • YouTube (read-only): the list of videos the signed-in account has liked, using the youtube.readonly permission. samsync cannot post, comment, like, subscribe, upload or change anything on YouTube.
  • Raindrop.io: it adds bookmarks (video link and title) to the person's own Raindrop account using a token that person provides.

Where data is stored

The Google access token and a list of already-saved video IDs are stored only on a computer Sam controls. Nothing is stored on any other server, and the data is not used for advertising or analytics.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with anyone. Video links go only to the person's own Raindrop.io account.

Removing access

You can revoke samsync's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can delete the Raindrop token at app.raindrop.io/settings/integrations. If you ask, the locally stored data is deleted.

samsync's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
